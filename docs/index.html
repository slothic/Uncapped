Uncapped.

A free, community-run server for a classic online RPG.

Uncapped is a hobby project — a small, heavily customised game server with reworked dungeons, crafting, and progression systems, played by a handful of people who like tinkering with it. It is run by one person in their spare time, and it is free to play. There is no shop, no subscription, and nothing behind a paywall.

Supporting the server

Running the realm costs money: a rented server, and the bandwidth to keep it online around the clock. Players sometimes ask whether they can chip in toward that bill. This page is the answer.

What a donation is — and isn't:

  • Entirely voluntary. The server is free and stays free whether or not anyone donates.
  • Nothing is sold. You are not buying goods, services, items, currency, or access.
  • No perks, no advantage. Donating grants no in-game benefit, rank, or status of any kind. Donors and non-donors play exactly the same game.
  • It goes to hosting. Contributions offset server and hosting costs. They do not currently cover them.

Support the server

Please don't donate money you need. This is a hobby that a few people enjoy — it is not worth anyone going short over.

Refunds & contact

Since donations are voluntary gifts rather than purchases, there is nothing to deliver and nothing to return. That said, if you donated by mistake or changed your mind, get in touch and it will be refunded, no questions asked.

Questions, refund requests, or anything else: slothic@live.dk, or find us on Discord.
